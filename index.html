Inputs

Guardsmen Select 3 Guardsmen, or 2 when using a Specialist
Recommended: For better Specialist results, use G7 or higher as your main Guardsmen tier. If your available Guardsmen are below G7, use Guardsmen Only instead.
Specialists Requires full Modernisation + 2 Guardsmen tiers
Why Specialists may be greyed out: Specialists require full Army Modernisation research and cannot be used alone. Select 2 valid Guardsmen tiers first; the compatible Specialist options will then become available.
Monsters Select 2 or 3 consecutive Monster tiers
Recommended build: 20 stacks. Use 3 consecutive Guardsmen tiers, or 2 consecutive Guardsmen plus 1 valid Specialist when full Army Modernisation research is complete. Specialists cannot be used alone. Select 2 or 3 consecutive Monster tiers.
How to use:
1. Enter your Leadership, Authority and Dominance.
2. Choose your Stack Mode first. For Specialist builds, select 2 valid Guardsmen tiers before selecting a Specialist.
3. Specialists require full Army Modernisation research and cannot be used without a Guardsmen backbone.
4. The exact Leadership entered is used. Final troop counts are converted to whole units.
Specialist stacks require full Army Modernisation research.
Troop Calculator Disclaimer
This calculator provides a baseline setup to guide troop composition and firing order. Due to differences in player bonuses, research, gear, and other modifiers, results may vary. Players are encouraged to fine-tune and adjust their setup to achieve optimal performance based on their individual account.
